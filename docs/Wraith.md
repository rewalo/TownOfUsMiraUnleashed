# Wraith

![Wraith](assets/RoleHeaders/Wraith.png)

- **Team:** Impostor
- **Alignment:** Impostor Power
- **Colour:** Impostor red

## Overview

The Wraith is an Impostor Power role focused on fast, unpredictable movement. Dash increases your movement speed by 75% for a short time. Lantern lets you place a hidden marker only you can see; reactivate it to teleport back and briefly turn invisible. If the Lantern expires before you return, it breaks and leaves permanent evidence visible to all players.

## Abilities

| Ability | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Dash | Temporarily increase your movement speed by 75%. |
| Lantern | Place a Lantern only you can see. Reactivate it to teleport back and briefly turn invisible. If it expires, it breaks and leaves evidence visible to everyone. |

## Options

| Option | Range | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dash Cooldown | 10 – 60 s | 32.5 s |
| Dash Duration | 3 – 15 s | 6 s |
| Lantern | On / Off | On |
| Lantern Cooldown | 5 – 60 s | 37.5 s |
| Lantern Duration | 1 – 20 s | 10 s |
| Invisible Duration | 0 – 5 s | 2.5 s |

## Interactions & notes

- With [Perfect Comms](Voice-Chat) installed, an invisible Wraith is muted and hidden from the voice overlay until the invisibility ends.

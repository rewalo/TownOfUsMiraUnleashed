# Achievements

Mira Unleashed adds **58 achievements**, shown in their own **Mira Unleashed** tab of the Achievements browser. Progress for multi-step achievements is tracked automatically, and some persist across rounds or even across sessions.

Rarity tiers: **Common** -> **Rare** -> **Epic** -> **Legendary**.

> Three achievements are **hidden**: their names, artwork and requirements stay secret until you unlock them.

# Trapper

![Trapper](assets/RoleHeaders/Trapper.png)

- **Team:** Crewmate
- **Alignment:** Crewmate Support
- **Colour:** #A6D1B3 (crewmate blue when "Use Crewmate Team Color" is on)

## Overview

The Trapper is a Crewmate Support role that can place traps on vents. When players use a trapped vent, they are immobilized for a duration and the Trapper is notified.

## Abilities

| Ability | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Trap | Place a trap on a vent. Players who use this vent will be caught and immobilized. |

## Options

| Option | Range | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trap Cooldown | 1 – 60 s | 25 s |
| Trap Duration | 0.5 – 15 s | 4 s |
| Arrow Duration | 0.5 – 15 s | 4.5 s |
| Max Number of Traps | 1 – 15 | 3 |
| Trap Rounds Last | 0 – 15 | 3 |
| Get More Traps From Tasks | On / Off | On |
| Tasks Until More Traps | 1 – 10 | 2 |
| Trap Targets | Impostors / Impostors and Neutrals / All | Impostors and Neutrals |
| Arrow Target | Vent / Person | Vent |

## Interactions & notes

- In Mira Unleashed the Trapper sits in the **Crewmate Support** alignment (the old mod incorrectly used Crewmate Investigative).
- Do not confuse this role with TOU-Mira's own **Trapper** role — while Mira Unleashed is loaded, TOU's Trapper is displayed as **Revealer**.
- Players trapped in a vent are notified they were caught, and the Trapper is told which room the trap triggered in.

# Lawyer

![Lawyer](assets/RoleHeaders/Lawyer.png)

- **Team:** Neutral
- **Alignment:** Neutral Benign
- **Colour:** TOU Lawyer colour

## Overview

The Lawyer is a Neutral Benign role that wins by keeping their assigned client from being voted out. If their client gets voted out, the Lawyer loses.

## Abilities

| Ability | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Object | During meetings, click the objection button on your client's vote area to object to all votes, clearing them and forcing players to vote again. |

## Options

| Option | Range | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lawyer Win Mode | Win With Client / Steal Win | Win With Client |
| Killer Client Chance | 0 – 100 % | 70 % |
| On Client Death, Lawyer Becomes | Crewmate / Amnesiac / Survivor / Mercenary / Jester | Crewmate |
| Lawyer Dies When Client Dies | On / Off | Off |
| Lawyer Gets Voted Out With Client | On / Off | On |
| Lawyer Knows Client's Role | On / Off | On |
| Max Objections (Per Game) | 0 – 10 | 1 |
| Max Objections Per Meeting | 0 – 10 | 1 |
| Prevent Same Votes On Objection | On / Off | On |
| Lawyer/Client Gets A Private Chat | On / Off | On |

## Interactions & notes

- A lawyer (§) marker appears next to your client on the name plate, and a lawyer–client pair shows the marker on the end-game summary.
- Lawyer and client share a private team chat when the option is enabled; dead players can read it when "The Dead Know" is on.
- With **Win With Client**, the Lawyer wins whenever the client wins. With **Steal Win**, a living Lawyer + living Client at game end steal the win outright; the Lawyer can also end the game when only Lawyer + Client remain, or when a final-3 includes the duo without other killers.
- An Amnesiac who remembers a dead Lawyer inherits the remembered Lawyer's client.
- If the client dies (or is voted out without the voted-out option), the Lawyer either dies or becomes the configured role.
- Objections cannot be used in the last 20 seconds of voting.
- With [Perfect Comms](Voice-Chat) installed, the Lawyer and client get a private Team Radio channel when Team Radio is on — like the private chat, the client learns who their Lawyer is.

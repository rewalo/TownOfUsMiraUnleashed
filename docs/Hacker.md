# Hacker

![Hacker](assets/RoleHeaders/Hacker.png)

- **Team:** Impostor
- **Alignment:** Impostor Support
- **Colour:** Impostor red

## Overview

The Hacker is an Impostor Support role that can download information from nearby equipment to charge a portable device. Once you start downloading, you're locked into that equipment type until the next round. Jam disrupts information systems as if comms were sabotaged, but emergency meetings can still be called.

## Abilities

| Ability | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Download | While near a downloadable console (Admin/Cams/Vitals/Door Log), download to charge your battery. Every second downloading grants battery (configurable). You cannot switch equipment type until the next round. |
| Device | Use the downloaded system anywhere, consuming battery over time. |
| Jam | Spend a Jam charge to disrupt information systems for a duration. This behaves like comms being down, but does not prevent calling emergency meetings. |

## Options

| Option | Range | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Simple Mode (Jam Only) | On / Off | Off |
| Maximum Battery Duration | 3 – 15 s | 10 s |
| Battery Gained Per Download Second | 1 – 4 s | 2 s |
| Download Range | 0.5 – 2.5 | 1 |
| Move While Using Device | On / Off | On |
| Initial Jam Charges | 0 – 10 | 3 |
| Jam Charges Per Kill | 0 – 5 | 1 |
| Max Jam Charges | 1 – 10 | 6 |
| Jam Cooldown | 10 – 35 s | 25 s |
| Jam Duration | 5 – 20 s | 15 s |

## Interactions & notes

- Simple Mode disables Download/Device and leaves Jam as the only ability.

# Forestaller

![Forestaller](assets/RoleHeaders/Forestaller.png)

- **Team:** Crewmate
- **Alignment:** Crewmate Support
- **Colour:** #F1C40F (crewmate blue when "Use Crewmate Team Color" is on)

## Overview

The Forestaller is a Crewmate Support role that can prevent sabotages before they happen. After completing all tasks, sabotages are disabled while the Forestaller is alive. The Forestaller is revealed after finishing their last task.

## Options

| Option | Range | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Extra Short Tasks | 0 – 5 | 1 |
| Extra Long Tasks | 0 – 5 | 1 |
| Reveal Timing | Instant / Next Meeting | Instant |

## Interactions & notes

- Extra tasks are added on top of the normal task assignment.
- Sabotage blocking applies to all sabotage sources while the Forestaller lives.
- Reveal timing controls whether the Forestaller's role is shown to everyone immediately on task completion or at the next meeting.

# Voice Chat

Mira Unleashed integrates with [Perfect Comms](https://github.com/artriy/Perfect-Comms), a proximity voice chat mod for Among Us. The integration activates automatically when both mods are installed — nothing needs to be configured to turn it on.

## Host options

These toggles appear in the Perfect Comms host panel under the **Mira Unleashed** tab and are synced to every player in the lobby.

| Option | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wraith: Mute While Invisible | On | An invisible Wraith cannot transmit voice until the Lantern invisibility ends. |
| Hacker: Jam Disrupts Voice | On | Everyone's voice is muted during tasks while a Jam is active, like a comms sabotage. |
| Injector: Muffle Injected Hearing | On | Players injected with Low Vision, Very Low Vision, Confusion or Nausea hear muffled voice during tasks. |
| Team Radio - Lawyer | On | The Lawyer and their client share a private Team Radio channel when Team Radio is enabled. |

Invisible Wraiths are also hidden from the voice overlay so nearby players can't spot them talking.
